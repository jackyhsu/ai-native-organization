What is an AI-native organization?

An AI-native organization is not a traditional company with Copilot added. It is designed around AI agents, shared memory, parallel execution, and human oversight. Ingora, a Singapore company founded in 2026, is testing through its Shanghai subsidiary whether five humans can cover capabilities that might once have required 40–50 people.

This is a capability-surface experiment—not a claim that five people equal fifty-person productivity.

About Ingora

Ingora is a Singapore company experimenting with what it means to build an AI-native organization. At this early stage, access to AI talent and hardware supply chains matters, so many of the stories on this site take place inside its Shanghai subsidiary in China.

The experiment is led by Jacky Hsu. The outcome remains unknown; next, we will keep testing where humans should supervise agents operating at high speed and in parallel.
