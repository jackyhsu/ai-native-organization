# Contributing

English | 中文

Thank you for following the experiment. This repository is an authored record of a real company, so its contribution model is intentionally narrow.

## Where to participate

- Use [Discussions](https://github.com/jackyhsu/ai-native-organization/discussions) for questions, interpretations, and suggestions for what we should test next.
- Open an [Issue](https://github.com/jackyhsu/ai-native-organization/issues) for a factual correction, broken link, or mismatch between the English and Chinese versions.
- Please do not submit external stories, long-form essays, translations, promotional copy, or unsolicited rewrites as pull requests.

If a correction requires a text change, the maintainer may invite a focused pull request after the issue is reviewed.

## What a useful correction includes

- The exact page and sentence involved.
- The evidence or source supporting the correction.
- Whether the English version, Chinese version, or both are affected.
- A concise proposed correction that preserves the experiment's uncertainty.

## Content boundaries

Do not post private prompts, customer or employee information, non-public company documents, credentials, proprietary workflows, or confidential commercial information.

Please keep discussion specific, respectful, and evidence-oriented.

感谢你关注这场实验。由于本仓库记录的是一家真实公司的经历，参与范围会保持克制。

## 在哪里参与

- 关于实验的问题、理解与下一步建议，请使用 [Discussions](https://github.com/jackyhsu/ai-native-organization/discussions)。
- 事实错误、失效链接或中英文差异，请提交 [Issue](https://github.com/jackyhsu/ai-native-organization/issues)。
- 请不要直接提交外部故事、长篇文章、翻译稿、宣传文案或大篇幅改写。

如果纠错需要修改文字，维护者可能在核实 Issue 后邀请一个范围明确的 Pull Request。

## 有效纠错应包含

- 涉及的具体页面和句子。
- 支持修改的证据或来源。
- 英文、中文还是两个版本受到影响。
- 保留实验不确定性的简短修改建议。

## 内容边界

请勿发布私人 Prompt、客户或员工信息、未公开公司文件、凭据、专有工作流或保密商业信息。

讨论请保持具体、尊重事实，并尽可能提供证据。
