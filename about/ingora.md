# About Ingora

English | [中文](../zh-CN/about/ingora.md)

**By Jacky Hsu**

Ingora is a Singapore company experimenting with what it means to build an AI-native organization. At this early stage, access to AI talent and hardware supply chains matters, so many of the stories in this repository take place inside its Shanghai subsidiary in China.

The founding team has five people. Together they are testing whether AI agents, shared company memory, and new human–AI workflows can help a small team operate across a much wider capability surface than a conventional startup of the same size.

This public project focuses on the organizational experiment, not on confidential product details, customers, suppliers, or internal architecture.

Ingora was founded in 2026 by Jacky Hsu after nearly 25 years in the technology industry at Microsoft. The experience of moving from a large global technology company to a five-person startup is part of the experiment: assumptions that make sense at scale do not always survive first contact with a company that is being built from zero around AI.

The experiment is ongoing. Ingora does not yet know whether this model will be sustainable.
